Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Last updated: August 23, 2025

1. Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between you ("Customer") and Eazymode, Inc. ("Eazymode") and governs the processing of personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

2. Definitions

For the purposes of this DPA:

  • Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person
  • Processing: Any operation performed on personal data
  • Data Controller: The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data
  • Data Processor: The entity that processes personal data on behalf of the controller

3. Roles and Responsibilities

Customer acts as the Data Controller and Eazymode acts as the Data Processor for personal data processed through the Eazymode platform.

4. Processing Details

Subject Matter

Processing of personal data necessary for the provision of Eazymode's educational platform services.

Duration

Processing will continue for the duration of the service agreement and retention periods as specified in our Privacy Policy.

Nature and Purpose

Processing is necessary to provide chat-first educational programs, progress tracking, and related platform services.

Categories of Data

  • Contact information (name, email)
  • Account and authentication data
  • Educational progress and completion data
  • Communication data (chat messages, voice notes)
  • Usage and analytics data

Categories of Data Subjects

  • Platform administrators
  • Educators and instructors
  • Students and program participants

5. Data Security

Eazymode implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:

  • Encryption of personal data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security assessments and updates
  • Access controls and authentication measures
  • Staff training on data protection

6. Sub-processors

Eazymode may engage sub-processors to assist in providing services. Current sub-processors include:

  • Supabase (database hosting)
  • Stripe (payment processing)
  • Postmark (email services)
  • Twilio (messaging services)

7. Data Subject Rights

Eazymode will assist Customer in responding to data subject requests, including:

  • Access to personal data
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure of personal data
  • Data portability
  • Restriction of processing

8. Data Breach Notification

Eazymode will notify Customer without undue delay upon becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer's data.

9. International Transfers

Any transfer of personal data to third countries will be subject to appropriate safeguards as required by applicable data protection laws.
